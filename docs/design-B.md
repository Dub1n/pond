# pond deck design – option B (header + outriggers)

> 1 m walk + 0.5 m overhang; framed opening type

---

## overview

* pond: 3 m × 3 m × ≈ 0.9 m deep
* desired: 1 m walk-around, 0.5 m overhang into pond
* deck finish: ~100 mm above max water level
* priorities: maintain full walk width, clean liner clamp, robust overhang framing

---

## framing summary

**primary layout:**

* deck formed as a rectangular frame around a central 3×3 m opening (the pond).
* **side trimmers** (double joists) run parallel to pond edges.
* **headers** (double joists) sit 500 mm back from the cantilever edge and now extend 500 mm beyond each pond corner (overall run 4.0 m, from 500 mm to 4 500 mm on plan), so adjacent sides lap neatly and share corner posts.
* **walkway joists** (continuous 47×150s) run perpendicular to the pond. Each joist spans the outer beam to one of three bearing lines:
  * outer beam → cantilever edge (across the cantilever band),
  * outer beam → trimmer (over the corner shoulders),
  * outer beam → header (through the central walkway zone).
  Spacing stays 400 mm on centre everywhere, eliminating overlapping outrigger/backspan members.

**member sizes (C24 UC4-treated softwood):**

| member        | size             | spacing/notes                        |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| side trimmers | double 47×200 mm | run full pond length                 |
| headers       | double 47×200 mm | span between trimmers + 500 mm lap each end |
| walkway joists| 47×150 mm        | 400 mm c/c, continuous spans as above|
| rim blocking  | 47×150 mm        | install at cantilever edge + shoulders|
| deck boards   | 28×145 mm        | 5–6 mm gaps, 20–30 mm overhang       |

---

## joist support & cantilever connection

**connection type:**

* Continuous joists bear on outer beam, header, and trimmer as their span dictates; use **joist hangers** or **bolted bearing plates** at header/trimmer interfaces.
* **LSTA-type straps** remain at the cantilever edge to counter uplift, wrapping over each joist.
* Add **angle brackets or cantilever seats** under the joists at the header-bearing line where the span transitions to cantilever.
* **Blocking** sits flush with the cantilever edge and within shoulder bays to prevent roll and tie the picture-frame rim.

**load path:**

* decking load → joist → header/trimmer → outer beam/posts.
* cantilever uplift at the edge restrained by strap/seat combo.

**fasteners:**

* stainless A2/A4 or class 4 structural screws/bolts.
* pre-drill bolt holes to avoid splitting.

---

## side trimmers & headers

* double joists bolted or screwed together (spaced with 3–5 mm washer gap for drying).
* headers project past the trimmers by 500 mm each way so perpendicular headers meet; notch or scab at the overlap to keep the top arrises flush.
* connect headers to trimmers using **joist hangers** or **bolted bearing**.
* all ends supported by **posts or beams** at corners and midpoints.
* pads or ground screws at 1.5 m centres.
* DPC or EPDM between timber and concrete.

### edge framing

* Outer beam bears directly on perimeter posts; shim beam elevation so top arrises sit 20–30 mm above post tops, keeping joists seated fully.
* Picture-frame rim ties into joist blocking at the cantilever edge; maintain gaps for drainage.

---

## edge & liner interface

* liner folded up and over a soft underlay strip (pond underlay or 6–10 mm closed-cell foam).
* clamped with 45×45 mm treated batten using stainless screws at 150–200 mm centres.
* screw line ≥75 mm above max water level.
* optional **liner-lock strip** instead of batten.
* rim board or fascia hides clamp detail; underside drip kerf 10–15 mm in from edge.

---

## decking surface

* boards perpendicular to outriggers.
* 1–2% fall away from pond for drainage.
* picture-frame rim board around pond opening for clean finish and structural tie.
* 20–30 mm overhang past rim with drip kerf.

---

## materials & protection

* UC4-treated softwood; reseal all cut ends.
* stainless or class 4 hardware.
* breathable membrane under deck for weed suppression.
* isolate liner from metal with EPDM pads or underlay.

---

## connection summary

| function          | component                   | primary resistance    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------- |
| shear / bearing   | joists + hanger/seat        | timber bearing + steel brackets |
| uplift / peel     | LSTA strap over cantilever edge | tension            |
| roll / twist      | blocking at rim and shoulders| compression restraint |
| transfer to frame | bolts / structural screws   | shear + bearing       |
| corrosion         | stainless hardware          | durability            |

---

## build sequence (option B)

1. set pads/ground screws, install side trimmers and outer beam.
2. fix headers between trimmers at 500 mm back from pond edge.
3. install continuous joists at 400 mm centres, ensuring span transitions match bearing lines; add blocking and cantilever straps.
4. clamp liner to rim; add picture-frame fascia.
5. lay decking boards, maintain gaps and slope.
6. verify fixings tight, straps seated, and edges chamfered.

**notes:**
Option B provides a robust overhang with clear separation between walkway and pond frame. It trades some complexity (headers, outriggers, straps) for a wider clear walk and a stiffer cantilever. Ideal when the walk must stay narrow (~1 m) but the visual overhang wants to extend 0.5 m.
