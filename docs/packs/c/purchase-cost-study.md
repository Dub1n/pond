# Pack C rail — materials, purchases and cost position

## Current result

All hardware, cord and webbing has been purchased. The only outstanding
structural purchases are the aluminium plate and GRP rail. The former
GBP 552.29 split-GRP total is historical and must not be used as the current
order value: obtain delivered quotations for the aluminium and 3.2 mm-wall
rail before stating a new total.

The current prototype comprises:

- five 670 x 460 x 10 mm EN AW-5083 aluminium sheets, H temper to be confirmed;
- two 6 m lengths of 38 x 38 x 3.2 mm pultruded GRP hollow section;
- nine one-piece R5.3 seat uppers and eighteen lower bridges;
- four one-piece corner-v3 uppers and four lower webs;
- the purchased suspension, connection and thirteen basket-retention sets.

The pond is ordinary freshwater with no deliberately added salt. That does
not remove the need to confirm water chemistry, the aluminium temper and wet
compatibility, or to isolate aluminium from stainless hardware. Zinc-plated
steel, brass and unidentified coatings remain unacceptable submerged
substitutes.

The conservative sum of these rectangular envelopes is 1.2049 m2. Five stock
sheets provide 1.5410 m2. Four provide only 1.2328 m2—2.3% above the envelope
sum before kerf, edge margin or the actual non-rectangular nest—so **five
sheets remain the minimum purchasing allowance** unless a cutter demonstrates
a valid four-sheet nest from the exact SVGs.

Using one-piece uppers removes the 57-part split arrangement, its upper-ply
interfaces and the corner bearing step. It retains the current holes,
fasteners, POM-C sleeves, corner crush sleeves and separate C2 posts, so no
additional stainless components are introduced.

### Material and thickness basis

The available stock is nominal 10 mm EN AW-5083, but its H temper is unknown.
5083 is a reasonable freshwater prototype candidate subject to certificate,
water chemistry, stainless isolation, drainable cleanable interfaces and
immersed coupon/complete-joint checks. Ten millimetres is the selected stock
thickness, not a calculated minimum for an unidentified temper.

The earlier elastic screens show substantial gross-plate margin at 10 mm:
about 6.0 MPa and 0.05 mm for a 40 mm seat arm at the deliberately
conservative 0.30 kN load; about 7.2 MPa and 0.006 mm for a 35 mm lower bridge;
and about 30 MPa for the former 75 mm C2 strip comparison. This indicates
future thinning may be possible, but does not justify trimming the selected
profiles or buying thinner plate. The real controls are certified temper,
M8/M6 open-hole and bearing behaviour, basket bearing, corner stiffness,
corrosion detailing and completed wet tests.

Do not credit paint, anodising or sealant alone as corrosion protection at a
damaged or permanently wet fastener interface. Deburr and lightly break every
edge and hole, remove cutting residue, maintain electrical isolation and
ensure water cannot be trapped in a faying surface.

## GRP rail substitution

Buy two 6 m lengths of [F.H. Brundle 38 x 38 x 3.2 mm grey GRP hollow
section](https://www.fhbrundle.co.uk/products/33033832GY__GRP_Hollow_Section_38_x_38_x_3_2mm_x_6m_Grey), subject to current
availability, delivered price and product evidence.

The existing gross-member calculation gives the 3.2 mm section
`I = 90,668 mm4` and approximately `J = 134,861 mm4`, about 74% and 75% of the
former 5 mm-wall values. It remains plausible in the stated member screens,
including 13.2 MPa and 2.40 mm in the deliberate 0.30 kN / 840 mm support-loss
case.

This does not release the perforated connection. The thinner wall raises the
calculated two-wall bearing demand at the retained 0.763 kN corner action from
about 9 MPa to about 14 MPa, and the nominal cavity changes from 28.0 to
31.6 mm. Measure the delivered cavity, remake all 24 corner crush sleeves to
fit, and repeat the complete wet-corner proof before batch use.

thread transition toward the head.

## Purchased cord and webbing allocation

The 5 mm polyester allowance remains approximately 92.34 m including samples,
knots, tails and contingency. The separate 3 mm polyester is locating cord
only; it is not structural suspension. The 20 m tubular-webbing purchase
allocates approximately 9.00 m to upper timber contacts, 3.84 m to lower rail

samples and replacement.

## Procurement and release conditions

Before ordering plate or releasing batch cutting:

1. obtain the aluminium grade, H temper, thickness and traceable certificate;
2. confirm water chemistry, the stainless-isolation detail and an immersed
   coupon/complete-joint test plan;
3. obtain the rail's current price and wet-service/product evidence, then
   measure its delivered wall and cavity;
4. overlay real B23/B27 basket ribs and all washer footprints on the exact SVGs;
5. prove one R5.3 seat and one complete perforated corner dry and after wet
   dwell using the cases in [install-walkthrough.md](install-walkthrough.md);
6. demonstrate the five-sheet cutting nest with cutter kerf and edge margins;
   and
7. complete flexible-ring and deck checks with measured geometry and reactions.

Fabrication is a prototype step, not structural approval. See
[install-walkthrough.md](install-walkthrough.md) for assembly, proof,
installation and inspection.
