# R5.3 — one-piece aluminium basket seats

## Status and scope

R5.3 is the current Pack C seat prototype. Nine mid-rail seats use one 10 mm
aluminium upper and two 10 mm aluminium lower bridges. It supersedes the split
GRP [R5.2](R5.2.md) arrangement while retaining its two capture stations,
four-bolt rail capture, sleeves, basket fasteners and proof envelope.

The cutting files and quantities are controlled by
[aluminium-cut-parts.md](aluminium-cut-parts.md). Fabrication and installation
are controlled by [install-walkthrough.md](install-walkthrough.md).

## Parts and interfaces

Each seat uses:

| Item                                   | Per seat | Nine seats |
| -------------------------------------- | -------: | ---------: |
| One-piece upper, `40mm-r512.svg`       |        1 |          9 |
| Lower bridge, `40mm-r512-bridge.svg`   |        2 |         18 |
| M8 bolt, 25 mm broad washers and nyloc |   4 sets |    36 sets |
| Flattened 20 OD x 10 ID POM-C sleeve   |        4 |         36 |
| M6 basket-retention set                |        4 |         36 |

The upper is 190 x 190 mm overall. Its four inner 8.5 mm holes form the two
rail-capture stations: 150 mm between stations and 56 mm across the rail. Its
four outer 6.6 mm holes are normal-clearance holes for the retained M6 basket
fasteners. A fabricator must not resize or reinterpret those holes.

Each lower bridge has a 40 x 100 mm drawing envelope, a 40 x 96 mm cut profile
and two 8.5 mm holes at 56 mm centres. The SVG is authoritative.

The available plate is nominal 10 mm EN AW-5083 with unknown H temper. Confirm
the alloy, temper, thickness and traceability before cutting. The unknown
temper means these profiles remain prototype parts rather than a minimum-
thickness release. Keep aluminium electrically isolated from stainless
hardware and avoid water-holding faying surfaces.

## Load paths

Basket gravity passes through the reinforced basket base into the one-piece
upper and then directly into the rail. The two lower bridges, four bolts and
four POM-C sleeves retain the seat under uplift and overturning and maintain
mechanical rail capture. Longitudinal restraint comes from the broad upper-to-
rail interface; no lashing slot or cord groove is used.

Plate flexure is not the controlling screen at 10 mm: the earlier deliberately
conservative 0.30 kN comparison gave about 6.0 MPa and 0.05 mm for a 40 mm
upper arm, and about 7.2 MPa and 0.006 mm for a 35 mm lower bridge. The retained
40 mm profiles instead protect basket bearing, washer lands, edge distance,
handling and corrosion-isolation space.

## Release gates

Before batch use, record:

- the real plate grade, temper, thickness and permanent-freshwater condition;
- an overlay of the real basket ribs, all washers and the exact SVG holes;
- completed bolt grips, including the plain-shank/thread transition, nyloc
  engagement and any trimmed threaded excess;
- 0.30 kN centred, 50 mm eccentric, local bearing and uplift tests, dry and
  after representative wet dwell;
- 100 N longitudinal action in both directions with no more than 1 mm
  permanent migration and no progressive slip; and
- empty-basket retention, one-fastener-loss and complete removal trials.
