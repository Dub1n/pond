# Pack C corner v3 — one-piece aluminium corner seat

## Status and scope

Corner v3 is the current corner prototype for the 2,100 mm-centreline ring. It
uses one 10 mm aluminium upper and one 10 mm aluminium lower web per corner. It
supersedes the split-GRP [corner v2](corner-v2.md), eliminating the upper-plate
slip interface and the stepped basket-bearing plane without changing the six
rail axes, separate C2 posts or purchased fastener counts.

The exact cutting files and quantities are controlled by
[aluminium-cut-parts.md](aluminium-cut-parts.md).

## Components and geometry

Each of four corners uses:

| Item                                                           | Quantity per corner |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------: |
| One-piece upper, `40mm_web.svg`                                |                   1 |
| Lower web, `corner-web-lower.svg`                              |                   1 |
| M8 rail bolt, two 25 mm washers, nyloc and fitted crush sleeve |              6 sets |
| Separate M8 x 50 C2 post, nylon spacer and washer stack        |              2 sets |
| M6 basket-retention set                                        |              4 sets |

The upper drawing is 380 x 380 mm and contains twelve nominal 8 mm holes: six
rail holes on the two 55/125/195 mm chains, two separate C2-post holes and four
basket-retention holes. The latter use the retained M6 hardware in the
as-drawn clearance holes. The 240 x 240 mm lower web contains the same six
rail axes as 8.5 mm holes. Do not add a bolt at the rail-axis intersection.

The available plate is nominal 10 mm EN AW-5083 with unknown H temper. Confirm
grade, temper, thickness and traceability before cutting. Electrically isolate
stainless hardware, deburr every edge and hole, and keep interfaces drainable
and inspectable.

The proposed 38 x 38 x 3.2 mm GRP rail has a nominal 31.6 mm cavity. Measure
every relevant delivered section and make the 24 internal crush sleeves to the
actual cavity; do not reuse the former 5 mm-wall sleeve length.

## Load paths and proof

Basket gravity bears through the single upper into both rails. Each C2 post has
a continuous aluminium path to its neighbouring 55 mm rail axis. The lower web
and all six sleeved rail bolts close the corner load path. The one-piece upper
must not be credited with stiffness until the complete perforated, wetted
joint has been tested.

One complete corner must demonstrate, after bedding:

- at least 20 kN m/rad stiffness from 0 to 60 N m in plan;
- the retained 30 N m roll case;
- realistic eccentric basket action;
- deliberately unequal C2-leg action and complete loss of one C2 sling; and
- no progressive slip, hole ovalisation, aluminium deformation, rail-wall
  crushing, corrosion-isolation damage or loss of drainage after wet dwell.

Use full-size B23/B27 templates with posts, roof washers, cord, chafe, knots,
cartridge and removal travel present before assigning a corner basket.
