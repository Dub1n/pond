Fabrication requirements:

- Treat every SVG path and hole as finished geometry at 1 SVG unit = 1 mm.
- Preserve every hole exactly as drawn: seat and corner basket holes are 6.6 mm for M6; M8 holes remain as drawn. Do not infer diameters from stroke width, resize clearance holes or add centre/intersection holes.
- Nest without distorting, mirroring or non-uniform scaling. Rotation in the plate plane is permitted.
- Remove swarf and cutting-fluid residue. Do not apply paint, anodising or sealant unless separately instructed.
- *optional* Deburr and lightly break every cut edge and both faces of every hole.
