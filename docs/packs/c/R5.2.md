# R5.2 — split-plate basket seats

## Status and scope

R5.2 is the current seat family for Pack C. Nine mid-rail seats use the split
five-part cradle below; four corners use the related split seat integrated into
[corner-v2.md](corner-v2.md). R5.1 is retained as design history and is not the
current cutting schedule.

This document defines the parts, interfaces, load paths and proof envelope.
All fabrication, assembly, basket modification, lowering, adjustment and test
procedure is consolidated in [install-walkthrough.md](install-walkthrough.md).

## Mid-rail seat

Each of the nine seats contains five 9.5 mm structural-GRP capsules:

- two transverse upper arms supporting reinforced basket-base regions;
- one upper bridge running along and bearing on the rail; and
- two lower bridges, one at each capture station.

All five parts have full semicircular ends. Overall length equals the relevant
connection-centre span plus one part width. Four M8 bolts pass through an upper
arm, the common upper bridge and a lower bridge, with one bolt on each side of
the rail at each capture station. Each arm has two bolts and cannot rotate
freely; the four-bolt upper bridge locks the two stations together.

The part order is upper arm, upper bridge, sleeve-controlled rail gap and lower
bridge. Broad GRP-to-GRP contact is retained between the upper parts; no loose
metal washer is placed between those layers.

## Three basket-base sets

The base dimension is the connection-centre span of an assumed continuous
reinforced square basket base, not its rim size. Select the smallest set that
fully supports the measured wet basket. Do not infer it from the B23 or B27
product name.

| Reinforced square base | Upper arms, each | Upper bridge | Lower bridges, each |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 250 x 250 mm | 40 x 290 mm | 90 x 340 mm | 35 x 91 mm |
| 225 x 225 mm | 40 x 265 mm | 90 x 315 mm | 35 x 91 mm |
| 200 x 200 mm | 40 x 240 mm | 90 x 290 mm | 35 x 91 mm |

Dimensions are width x overall length. The lower-bridge bolt-centre span stays
56 mm, so its 35 + 56 = 91 mm overall length is common to all three sets.

The 40 mm arm width is governed by useful basket bearing and connection margin,
not gross flexure. The 90 mm bridge contains bolt rows 56 mm apart with about
17 mm transverse centre-to-edge distance and passes the full 0.30 kN point-
load comparison even for the 250 mm span. One 35 mm lower bridge screens at
about 8.0 MPa and 0.055 mm movement when the complete 0.30 kN action is placed
on it.

## Fastener and rail interface

Each mid-rail seat uses:

| Item | Per seat | Nine seats |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| M8 x 90 A2-70 part-thread bolts | 4 | 36 |
| M8 25 mm broad A2 washers | 8 | 72 |
| M8 A2 nylocs | 4 | 36 |
| Bored 16 mm OD unfilled acetal sleeves | 4 | 36 |
| M6 basket-retention sets | 4 | 36 |

The selected M8 x 90 has a 68 mm plain shank. At 9.5 mm plate thickness, the
nominal distance to the lower nut-bearing washer is approximately 68 mm:
two upper plies, the measured 38 mm rail/gap, one lower ply and the washer.
The actual rail, sealed plate thicknesses, sleeve-controlled gap and washer
stack govern the final grip.

The four acetal sleeves sit beside the rail. They retain transverse location,
limit closure and define the measured clamp gap; they are not gravity columns
and must not be shortened merely to obtain a high bolt preload. The historical
39 mm length is a trial starting point, not an issued batch dimension.

## Load paths

Normal gravity passes from the reinforced basket base into both upper arms,
then by direct GRP bearing into the upper bridge and rail. The lower bridges
are normally unloaded. They engage under uplift, pitch or overturning and,
with the four bolts, maintain mechanical capture even if longitudinal grip is
lost.

Longitudinal restraint comes first from the broad upper-bridge/rail clamp.
A broad thin wet-compatible pad may be added if the plain interface fails the
slide test. A separately qualified immersed-service adhesive may supplement
the clamp, but never replaces the four bolts, sleeves or lower bridges.

Four distributed M6 positions retain the empty rigid basket. The 40 mm internal
washer bears on sound basket ribs; the 18 mm underside washer bears on GRP.
Normal planted load remains bearing load rather than bolt suspension.

## Material take-off

Across nine mid-rail seats, R5.2 needs:

- 18 upper arms;
- 9 upper bridges; and
- 18 lower bridges.

Together with eight corner-v2 upper halves and four thick lower webs, all three
basket-size cases have demonstrated orientation-controlled Deepnest layouts on
three 200 x 9.5 x 3,000 mm GRP flat bars. The cut-envelope totals are 1.1080,
1.0698 and 1.0315 m2 for the 250, 225 and 200 mm cases respectively. The
purchase quantity remains three bars in each case; smaller seats produce more
usable offcut rather than reducing the demonstrated order.

See [split-plate-stock-study.md](../../calcs/split-plate-stock-study.md) and the
retained layouts in `nesting/output/split-flat-bar/`.

## Release gates

R5.2 remains a prototype until the following pass:

- real B23 and B27 reinforced base/band survey and selection of each seat set;
- supplier material evidence, fibre orientation and sealed wet coupons;
- full washer bearing, bolt grip, sleeve compression and hidden-surface check;
- 0.30 kN centred, 50 mm eccentric, uplift and deliberately localised bearing
  cases, dry and after the wet dwell;
- 100 N longitudinal action in both directions with no more than 1 mm
  permanent migration and no progressive slip;
- empty-basket retention, one-fastener-loss, cartridge clearance and complete
  removal trials; and
- full-side and ring/deck checks using measured drained basket masses and
  actual suspension coordinates.
